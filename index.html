About Me

UX/UI & Product Designer | Graphic Design | Coding & Marketing Background.

I build digital products and visual systems that make complex workflows simple, intuitive, and engaging. With a degree in Graphic Design, a minor in Marketing, and 5+ years of hands-on experience spanning UX/UI, front-end code, and brand strategy, I bridge the gap between creative design, business goals, and technical execution.

My end-to-end process covers every stage of the product lifecycle—from user research and wireframing to high-fidelity design systems, interactive Figma prototypes, and front-end implementation. By integrating AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) into my workflow, I accelerate rapid prototyping and iterate based on real user feedback and analytics. Having lived in different cities and countries and experienced diverse cultures, I’m inspired by the many ways people interact with spaces, technology, and each other.
